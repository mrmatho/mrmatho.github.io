Pre-trip Practical notes for Day 1 (Fri 16)▼
Tipping
Never in Japan. It can be considered rude. No exceptions.
Shoes
Slip-ons ideal for temple days (frequent shoe removal). Day 6 (Wed 21) is the longest on foot — best walking shoes.
Konbini (convenience stores)
7-Eleven, FamilyMart and Lawson — hot food, fresh onigiri, good coffee. 7-Eleven ATMs accept foreign cards reliably.
Eki stamps (駅スタンプ)
Free rubber stamps unique to each station. Usually near ticket gates — ask staff "eki sutampu?" and hold up your notebook. Keep eki stamps separate from any goshuin book (temple calligraphy stamps).
