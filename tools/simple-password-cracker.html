How does a brute-force attack work?

A brute-force attack means trying every single possible password, starting with the shortest, until one of them matches. It's not clever — it just tries everything, one guess after another.

  • A 4-character password using only lowercase letters: about 456,976 combinations to try.
  • An 8-character password mixing capitals, lowercase, numbers and symbols: over 6 quadrillion combinations!
  • The more character types you use, and the longer your password is, the harder it becomes to crack.
  • A long password (like a whole made-up sentence) is much stronger than a short "clever-looking" one.
